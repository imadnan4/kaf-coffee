The KAF experience

A little piece of the Middle East in F-6

Tucked inside Roomy Signature Hotel on Agha Khan Road, KAF is Islamabad’s home for قهوة مختصة — speciality coffee. It’s an authentic, artisanal Arabic coffee and tea experience with eclectic Middle Eastern flavours, from slow-brewed Turkish coffee to cardamom-scented qahwa served the traditional way with dates.

The room balances elegance with comfort — earthy tones, Arabic details and a terrace that comes alive at night — a well-known hub for the city’s Arab community and for anyone serious about a good cup.

“Brewed fresh. Served right.”— @kaf_pk
